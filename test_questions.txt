========================================================================
 ShlokRAG — QA TEST BATTERY
 Run these at http://localhost:8000 (or the deployed link).
 Each section notes WHAT TO LOOK FOR.
========================================================================

------------------------------------------------------------------------
A. CORE THEMATIC QUESTIONS  (the app's strength)
   Look for: coherent grounded answer with [verse-ID] citations + relevant verses.
------------------------------------------------------------------------
1.  What is my duty in life?
2.  How do I control my mind and senses?
3.  What happens to the soul after death?
4.  How should I act without attachment to results?
5.  What is the nature of the self?

------------------------------------------------------------------------
B. ENTITY QUESTIONS - "who is X"  (tests the entities index)
   Look for: a real character description; sources include ENT-... entries.
------------------------------------------------------------------------
6.  Who are the Pandavas?
7.  Who is Arjuna?
8.  Who is Bhishma?
9.  Who is Krishna?

------------------------------------------------------------------------
C. GLOSSARY QUESTIONS - "what is X"  (tests the glossary)
   Look for: a clear definition; sources include GLO-... entries.
------------------------------------------------------------------------
10. What is dharma?
11. What is karma?
12. What is moksha?
13. What is yoga?

------------------------------------------------------------------------
D. STRUCTURAL QUERIES  (metadata handler - instant, no LLM, no quota)
   Look for: an immediate count/list, no "Searching..." delay.
------------------------------------------------------------------------
14. How many verses are in the Gita?          -> should say 719
15. How many verses are there in total?
16. Get me the verses from chapter 2
17. Show me chapter 12

------------------------------------------------------------------------
E. BLENDED-KNOWLEDGE QUESTIONS  (tests the loosened prompt)
   Look for: helpful answer using general knowledge + cites verses where relevant.
------------------------------------------------------------------------
18. What is the Bhagavad Gita about?
19. What is the story of the Ramayana?

------------------------------------------------------------------------
F. EDGE CASES & ROBUSTNESS
   Look for: graceful handling - NOT a crash or a made-up answer.
------------------------------------------------------------------------
20. Off-topic:   What's the best pizza recipe?     -> "couldn't find relevant verses" (gate)
21. Gibberish:   asdfghjkl qwerty                  -> no relevant verses
22. Wrong domain: How do I fix my car engine?      -> no relevant verses
23. Very short:  duty        (then)  anger         -> should still retrieve sensibly
24. Devanagari:  धर्म        (or)    कर्म          -> tests Sanskrit-script queries
25. Multi-part:  What is dharma, and how does it relate to one's duty?
26. Empty:       click Ask with an empty box       -> should do nothing (no error)

------------------------------------------------------------------------
G. GROUNDING / HONESTY CHECKS  (the most important)
   Look for: it does NOT invent facts, and it cites.
------------------------------------------------------------------------
27. Out-of-scope fact: Who is the current Prime Minister of India?
                       -> must say the verses don't cover this / no results (NO hallucination)
28. Confirm citations like [BG3.35] appear in answers.
29. Confirm the Sources list shows real, on-topic verses you can verify.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
H. RATE-LIMIT BEHAVIOR  (know the free-tier ceiling)
   Look for: graceful degradation, not a hang.
------------------------------------------------------------------------
30. Ask 5-6 questions quickly in a row -> some may show
    "answer service is busy... see the verses below" within ~15s, sources still shown.
    (This is Gemini free-tier throttling - the reason to consider Groq before a public deploy.)
